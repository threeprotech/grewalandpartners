About the firm

Grewal & Partners Inc. / Grewal et Associés Inc. Accounting for owner-managed businesses, from a Mississauga office.

Built for entrepreneurs who want the numbers clear

We are a full-service accounting firm based in Mississauga, Ontario, providing comprehensive financial services to businesses across Canada. We specialize in helping entrepreneurs and growing companies achieve their financial goals.

Our firm combines traditional accounting expertise with modern business strategies to deliver practical solutions that drive results. We understand the challenges facing Canadian businesses and provide the guidance needed to navigate complex financial landscapes.

The work is the ordinary, essential kind: books that close, payroll that remits, returns that go in on time, and a financing or CRA file that is ready when someone asks for it. You get one principal to call.
